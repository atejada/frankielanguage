# About

## What is Frankie?

The Frankie Programming Language - A procedural language stitched from Ruby, Python, R and Fortran — with a built-in web server, SQLite, and zero dependencies.

## Who created Frankie?

Frankie has been designed, coded, vibe-coded, build, whatever by Anthropic's Claude and I (<a href="https://atejada.github.io/" target="_blank">Blag aka. Alvaro Tejada Galindo</a>)

## Why Frankie?

Because I wanted the power of Python with the syntax of Ruby, the elegance of Fortran and the statistics power of R.
